A new AI tool launches almost every week, and every one of them promises to change how you work. You don't need to try them all. You need a way to decide quickly.

## Start with the job, not the tool

Write down the task you want help with: "summarize customer calls," "draft weekly status reports," "clean up spreadsheets." A tool that's brilliant at something you never do is useless to you.

## Test with your own work

Demos are polished. Your real task isn't. Take something you've actually done, run it through two or three tools with the same prompt, and compare the results side by side.

## Score what matters

Keep a tiny scorecard and rate each tool from 1 to 5:

- **Quality** on your task
- **Privacy** and data handling
- **Cost** at the level you'd really use it
- **Fit** with the apps you already use
- **Ease**: would you still use it on a busy Tuesday?

## Don't switch every week

Pick one, use it for a month, and only revisit when something clearly better shows up for your task. Constant switching feels productive and mostly isn't.

## Try it today

Choose one task you do weekly. Run it in two tools, score each on three of the criteria above, and keep the winner.
