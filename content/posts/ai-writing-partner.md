title: "Using AI as a Writing Partner, Not a Ghostwriter"
date: 2026-10-21
description: "How to get real help from AI while keeping your own voice and your own ideas."

---

If you ask AI to write the whole thing, you'll get something smooth, generic and vaguely familiar. If you use it as a partner, you'll get better writing that still sounds like you.

## Where it helps most

- **Getting unstuck.** Ask for three possible openings or a rough outline.
- **Testing structure.** "Does this order make sense? What's missing?"
- **Tightening.** Paste a paragraph and ask for a shorter version, then take only what you like.
- **Playing the skeptical reader.** "What would a critical reader push back on?"

## Keep your voice

Write the opinion, the story and the specifics yourself. Those are the parts only you have. If you want AI to match your style, paste in a page of your own writing and say so.

## Ask for feedback, not a rewrite

"Give me three specific criticisms of this paragraph" teaches you more than "make this better," and you stay in control of the words.

## Edit hard

AI drafts tend to lean on stock phrases like "in today's fast-paced world" and to line up everything in neat groups of three. Cut those. Replace vague words with concrete ones. Read it aloud.

## Be honest about it

Some schools, publishers and employers have rules about AI help. Know them, and follow them.

## Try it today

Take a paragraph you wrote and ask for three specific criticisms. Fix two of them yourself.
