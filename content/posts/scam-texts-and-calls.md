Phishing isn't limited to email. Smishing is phishing by text message, and vishing is phishing by phone call. Both work because we tend to trust our phones.

## Common versions

- **The delivery text:** "Your package couldn't be delivered, confirm your address."
- **The unpaid toll or fine text** with a link to pay right away.
- **The bank fraud call:** someone claiming to be from your bank's fraud team.
- **The tech support call** about a "virus" on your computer.
- **The family emergency call,** sometimes using a cloned voice, asking for money fast.

## The one rule that beats most of them

**Don't act on the contact. Verify through a channel you control.** Hang up and call the number printed on your card or the company's official site. Open the app yourself instead of tapping a link.

## Other signals

- Caller ID can be faked, so don't trust it.
- Real banks don't ask for your full PIN or a one-time code.
- Never read out a verification code to anyone who contacted you.
- Pressure, secrecy and unusual payment methods like gift cards or crypto are red flags.

## Protect your family

Agree on a family code word. If someone calls in a panic asking for money, ask for the word.

## Report it

In the US, you can forward scam texts to 7726 (SPAM) and report fraud at reportfraud.ftc.gov.

## Try it today

Pick a code word with your family or close friends, and tell them why.
