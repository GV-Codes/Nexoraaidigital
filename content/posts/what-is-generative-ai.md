Generative AI is software that produces new text, images, audio or code based on patterns it learned from a huge pile of existing examples. When you ask it a question, it doesn't look the answer up in a database. It predicts what a good answer would look like, one small piece at a time.

## How it works, roughly

A text model learns by reading enormous amounts of writing and practicing one task: guess what comes next. Do that billions of times and it picks up grammar, facts, tone and even some reasoning patterns. When you type a prompt, it simply continues the text in the way that fits best.

## What it's good at

- Drafting and rewriting text
- Summarizing long material
- Brainstorming ideas
- Explaining a topic at whatever level you need
- Writing and explaining code

## What it isn't

It isn't a search engine, a calculator or a person. It doesn't know things the way you do, and it never hesitates the way you would when you're unsure. That's why it can state something wrong in exactly the same confident voice as something right. Some tools can search the web or run code, which helps, but underneath they're still pattern predictors.

## A useful mental model

Picture a very well-read intern who works fast, never gets tired and occasionally bluffs. You'd happily hand them a first draft. You wouldn't let them sign the contract.

## Try it today

Ask a chatbot to explain something you know really well, like your own job. Notice what it gets right, what it misses, and what it makes sound fancier than it is. That gap is where your judgment still matters.
