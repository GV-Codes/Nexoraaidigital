If your team is using AI without any shared rules, people are making up their own. That's how confidential data ends up in the wrong place. You don't need a 30-page policy. You need a clear page.

## The five rules

1. **Use approved tools.** List which AI tools are fine for work, and who to ask about new ones.
2. **Protect data.** Spell out what must never be pasted in: customer data, credentials, unreleased plans, anything under contract.
3. **A human checks everything.** No AI output goes to a customer, a regulator or the public without someone reading it and standing behind it.
4. **Say when AI helped.** Decide when disclosure is required, and make it the norm for anything external.
5. **Watch for bias.** Be extra careful when AI output describes people, such as hiring, performance or customer scoring. Check for stereotypes and unfair patterns.

## Make it work in real life

- Keep it to one page and put it where people will see it.
- Give examples of good and bad use. They stick better than principles.
- Review it every few months. The tools change quickly.
- Encourage people to share prompts and wins, so rules feel like help rather than restriction.

## Try it today

Draft your own five rules in ten minutes, send them to two teammates, and ask what you missed.
