That update notification you keep dismissing is often a security fix. When a flaw is found in software, the company patches it, and the update announcement tells everyone, including attackers, where the weakness was. The window between the patch and your install is when you're most exposed.

## What to keep updated

- Your phone and computer operating systems
- Your web browser and its extensions
- Apps, especially messaging, banking and email
- Your home router
- Smart home devices

## Make it automatic

Turn on automatic updates wherever you can. Then restart when your device asks. Many updates don't finish until you do.

## Clear out what you don't use

Every app is another thing to keep patched. Uninstall what you've abandoned. Also watch for software that has reached end of life, meaning the maker no longer issues fixes. That's a sign to replace it.

## Beware of fake updates

A pop-up on a website claiming "Your browser is out of date, click to update" is a common trick. Update only through your device's settings or the official app store.

## Try it today

Check your phone and computer for pending updates, install them, and switch on automatic updates for both.
