Email and meetings eat a surprising amount of the week. AI won't fix that, but it can take the dull parts off your plate.

## Three jobs it does well

**Drafting replies from your bullets.** Jot down what you want to say in rough notes, then ask for a polite, short email. You supply the substance, it handles the phrasing.

**Summarizing long threads.** Paste in a 40-message chain and ask: "Summarize this in five bullets, then list any open questions and who owns them."

**Turning a transcript into next steps.** This one is the real time saver:

```
Here is a meeting transcript. List:
1. Decisions made
2. Action items, with the owner and due date if mentioned
3. Open questions
Keep it under 150 words.
```

## Cautions

- **Check names, dates and commitments.** AI sometimes assigns a task to the wrong person.
- **Ask before recording.** Get everyone's consent before you transcribe a meeting.
- **Mind confidentiality.** Use an approved tool for anything sensitive.
- **Make it sound like you.** Read the draft aloud. If you'd never say it, change it.

## Try it today

After your next meeting, paste your own rough notes into the prompt above and see how close the summary gets. Fix anything it got wrong and send it out.
